# Dashboard reference research

Live research completed 2026-09-30 for the requested polished creative studio redesign. Product truth was checked against `PRODUCT.md` and `DESIGN.md`: local narrated vertical-video workflow, Inter, Lucide, a single blue accent, light/dark/system themes, no invented library content or metrics.

## References inspected

1. [ClipPilot — AI Video Editor Dashboard, Potara Studio](https://dribbble.com/shots/27106290-ClipPilot-AI-Video-Editor-Dashboard). Opened in the live browser and inspected the enlarged design image. A compact navigation rail gives way to a media bin, large central portrait preview, and contextual enhancement panel. Playback and action accents use bright blue against graphite/black panel tiers. Hairline borders, thumbnail grids, and an anchored timeline establish a workbench hierarchy. Borrow the restrained panel structure and media prominence, not the sample assets or AI optimization claims.

2. [DuoVid AI Dashboard (Light & Dark Mode), JMJ Studio](https://dribbble.com/shots/27165633-DuoVid-AI-Dashboard-Light-Dark-Mode). Opened the live page and inspected its carousel images. The light and dark variants preserve the same compact navigation and thumbnail-library organization. Broad surfaces sit behind small media cards and concise filter/tool rows. Its green accent is not appropriate for ShortForge; the useful lesson is matched geometry and semantic colors across themes.

3. [Homepage for Video Editor Dashboard, Afterglow](https://dribbble.com/shots/21985476-Homepage-for-Video-Editor-Dashboard). Opened the live animated shot. The visible frame showed strong black/white editorial type, a blue primary control, and deliberate space around the main message. This supports a more confident page introduction, but its marketing-scale typography should be reduced for ShortForge's everyday workspace.

4. [UI — Dashboard (Darkmode), Pinterest board](https://kr.pinterest.com/liiiiiimiiiii/ui-dashboard-darkmode/). The live board loaded, exposing 42 pins and peripheral dashboard previews. A sign-in modal obscured the central content, so detailed pin claims were not inferred. Visible examples used dark panels and colorful chart highlights; those are weaker fits than the quieter studio references above. Pinterest was checked as requested, with this access limitation recorded.

5. [Cinemaro AI — Video Editor Dashboard SaaS, RonDesignLab](https://dribbble.com/shots/27400676-Cinemaro-AI-Video-Editor-Dashboard-SaaS). Verified the live page text. The designer describes a single canvas, persistent timeline, and contextual grading/effects panels. This was a written workflow reference, not a fully inspected visual reference; performance/business claims were not adopted.

## Translation into ShortForge

- Use a slim branded sidebar and compact utility header around a spacious media workspace. Group navigation by purpose and give the current destination a quiet blue-tinted highlight.
- Keep the light background visibly off-white with a restrained ivory-to-blue-gray gradient. Near-white elevated surfaces should remain distinguishable from the page without thick outlines.
- Use charcoal/navy tiers in dark mode, clear text contrast, and subtle edges. Retain the same layout and control hierarchy in both themes.
- Give Library a composed creative-studio introduction and one prominent import/create action. An empty collection needs an intentional visual and a practical next step, not fictional project cards or synthetic analytics.
- Keep content width, consistent spacing, typography, and alignment responsible for most of the polish. Use blue only for primary actions, selection, focus, and limited decorative emphasis.
- In Studio, prioritize the portrait canvas and readable contextual controls. Do not add marketing panels or decorative charts that consume editing space.
- Keep local/private status factual and small. Technical setup belongs in Settings and should not dominate the creative flow.

These recommendations are original synthesis of the references, not copied layouts or assets. No reference images were installed into the app.
